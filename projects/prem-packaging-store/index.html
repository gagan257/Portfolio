The project

Overview

store.prempackaging.com is Prem Industries India Limited's online store for packaging: corrugated boxes, paper bags, poly bags and its PACKPRO and ROLLABEL ranges, including boxes for sellers on marketplaces such as Amazon, Flipkart and Ajio.

I built it as a full-stack platform: the store customers shop on, the APIs behind it, and a custom CMS the team uses to manage catalog, inventory, orders and fulfilment.

My part

What I Built

  • The storefront

    The product catalogue, cart and checkout flows that customers use to buy packaging online.

  • A custom CMS

    A React admin dashboard for the team to manage catalog, inventory, orders and fulfilment.

  • REST APIs

    Node.js and Express APIs that the platform runs on.

  • Payments

    Razorpay, integrated through its webhooks.

  • Deployment

    Deployed and running on AWS EC2 and Amplify.

What a customer can do today

  • Browse by category, sort the results and search for a product.
  • Filter boxes by length, breadth and height, in inches or millimetres, and by brand.
  • Choose a pack size, check stock, and add to the cart or a wishlist.
  • Sign up, sign in and follow past orders.
  • Apply a promo code at checkout.
  • Send an enquiry for custom packaging.

These screens are from the public store. The admin dashboard and the source code belong to the company and are not public.
